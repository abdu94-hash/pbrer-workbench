Observed vs expected

Book: Chapter 13, Step 4

Inputs

The CI treats observed cases as Poisson and the expected count as fixed. Reports are subject to under-reporting, so observed counts are usually a lower bound; background rates often come from different populations and definitions. Use O/E to calibrate, not to conclude.

Two-arm trial comparison

Book: Chapters 10, 13, 17

Inputs

Relative risk with log-scale 95% CI; risk difference with Wald 95% CI; number needed to harm = 1 / risk difference. With few events, prefer exact methods and report counts.

About this workbench

Companion to The PBRER Handbook

The workbench implements the calculators and worksheets described in The PBRER Handbook. Each module names the chapter and template it supports. The demo buttons load the book's fictional composite cases so you can check your understanding against the worked examples.

All calculations run in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; use Print all modules (or your browser's print to PDF) to keep a record.
